Code of Conduct

Kept in sync with CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md in the repository

# Our Pledge

To foster an open and welcoming contribution environment, the EasySSH project pledges that no participant will be harassed, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

The maintainers pledge to govern the project with openness, inclusiveness, and professionalism.

# Our Standards

Encouraged Behavior

  • Use friendly, inclusive language and respect contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels
  • Give constructive feedback on others' contributions and acknowledge valuable ideas and work
  • Accept criticism gracefully and, in disagreements, focus on facts and technical substance
  • Be patient with newcomers and happy to share knowledge and experience
  • Provide complete, accurate information when reporting issues so others can reproduce and diagnose them

Unacceptable Behavior

  • Sexualized language, personal attacks, or insulting remarks
  • Harassment, trolling, or deliberately poisoning the tone of discussions
  • Publishing others' private information (such as addresses or contact details) publicly or privately without their consent
  • Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
  • Using project channels to spread advertising, spam, or content unrelated to the project

# Responsibilities

  • Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the scope of these standards and for taking appropriate, fair corrective action against unacceptable behavior (warnings, temporary or permanent removal of contributor status, etc.).
  • Contributors are obliged to follow this code of conduct and are accountable for their own words and actions.

# Scope

This code of conduct applies across all official project spaces (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussion forums), as well as to conduct in public settings when representing the project.

# Enforcement and Appeals

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through the contact channels published by the project maintainers (including the email channel in SECURITY.md). All complaints will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be protected. Maintainers may take whatever measures they deem appropriate against those who violate this code of conduct.

This code was originally written in Chinese in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, adapted to the specifics of the EasySSH project.

EasySSH

A portable SSH/SFTP terminal for Windows. Lightweight, self-developed implementation inspired by the SecureCRT experience, with 100% original code.
